Greenhouse

Greenhouse is a hiring platform whose marketing site presents itself as an end-to-end, AI-powered recruiting solution spanning sourcing to onboarding, anchored by the serif display line 'The only hiring platform you'll ever need' set in a deep forest green (#15372c) on a mint canvas (#cdeadf). The visual system pairs the Untitled Serif family for editorial headings with Untitled Sans for body and controls, uses rounded pill buttons in a primary green (#008561) and a blue demo-request accent (#3574d6), and closes pages on a dark green footer band with a 'Modern Recruiter newsletter' signup and a lowercase sprout 'g' wordmark.
