## Cross-page Evidence

### Structural Patterns
- **Body canvas pattern:** Dominant warm cream canvas; alternated with deep dark-brown full-bleed bands (hero, visit section) and a near-black footer tier. Two-tone alternation is the page's primary rhythm.
- **Section wrapper pattern:** Dominant: full-bleed colored wrappers with content inset inside. Exception: one full-bleed photographic band with no container at all.
- **Container pattern:** Dominant: wide container with generous side margins; content frequently uses only part of the container (narrow paragraph columns around one-third width, headings spanning one-half to two-thirds). Exceptions: centered narrow column for subscribe and footer.
- **Alignment pattern:** Dominant: left-aligned text with staggered horizontal anchor points per module. Exceptions: centered hero title, centered subscribe section, centered footer.
- **Spacing rhythm pattern:** Dominant: very large inter-module gaps on cream editorial runs; dark sections slightly denser; media bands break rhythm at full bleed.
- **Spacing mechanics:** Macro: section padding around 110px on light sections, around 100px on dark; module gaps within editorial runs around 120px. Micro: caption-to-image gaps around 12px; eyebrow-to-heading around 24px; price/list rows around 36px tall with hairline dividers; body paragraphs narrow with roughly 1.5 line-height.
- **Section transition pattern:** Dominant: hard surface resets between cream and dark bands. Notable exception: the hero-to-about boundary is bridged by an overlapping photo straddling the seam.
- **Run edge evidence:** Hard resets confirmed at hero/about (color flip, softened by bridging image), about/gallery-photo, photo/history, history/visit, visit/subscribe, subscribe/footer — all abrupt fill changes with no fades, rules, or gradients.

### Layout Patterns
- **Scaffolding logic:** Dominant: loose one-column editorial collage stacks (image + caption + paragraph modules at varied anchors). Secondary: centered single-column stacks (hero title, subscribe, footer). Occasional: two-cell split panel (ticket module) and overlay composite (map + card).
- **Within-scaffold layout logic:** Dominant: large serif heading block, then media rectangle, with a narrow paragraph offset beside or below the media; tiny uppercase metadata captions placed in adjacent negative space. Exception: ruled list rows inside the pricing panel.
- **Placement behavior:** Dominant: staggered/offset placement; common: deliberate overlap (title over hero image, image over image, card over map, content over ghost watermarks); occasional strict on-grid (footer, subscribe).
- **Breakout motifs:** Full-bleed photo band; boundary-straddling image at the hero seam; info card extending past its parent map's bottom edge.
- **Layout tension:** Medium-high — heavy use of asymmetric anchors, overlaps, and watermark layering, but contained by a calm two-tone canvas and very generous whitespace.

### Visual Patterns
- **Surface family evidence:** Three flat surface tiers: cream (dominant), deep warm brown (common, hero + visit), near-black brown (footer one-off). Child panels are cream/off-white. No gradients, no rounded corners, no shadows observed anywhere — everything is hard-edged flat rectangles.
- **Surface relationship evidence:** High-contrast children only: cream panels on dark brown (info card, ticket panel) separated purely by fill contrast — no borders or shadows. No same-surface cards observed, so no border-separation condition exists; borders appear only as hairline row dividers inside the pricing list and as the form underline.
- **Typography evidence:**
  - Display/page heading: very large high-contrast serif/didone-style face, all caps, thin hairline strokes with strong modulation, tall narrow letterforms, used for hero title and footer sitemap.
  - Section/editorial headings: same serif family at mid-display sizes, all caps, roughly 1.3 line-height, multi-line blocks.
  - Eyebrows/labels/metadata: tiny uppercase sans with wide tracking ("ABOUT", "VISIT", "ADDRESS", image captions).
  - Body: small low-contrast humanist sans, sentence case, roughly 1.5 line-height, narrow measure.
  - Control text: small uppercase tracked sans (nav links, BUY TICKETS, SUBSCRIBE).
  - Ghost watermark: the display serif at enormous scale, approx. 0.06 opacity.
  - Counter "1/6": display serif at medium size.
- **Component recipe evidence:** Eyebrow label = plain uppercase text, content-hugging, no pill/fill. Image module = hard-edged photo + margin micro-caption + narrow paragraph. Info card = cream panel, labeled subgroups, label-value rows. Price row = label left, arrow link right, hairline divider, parent-stretched across the panel. Form = full-width-of-column underline input with inline text submit. No filled buttons observed anywhere on the page.
- **Link/action role evidence:** (1) Header nav: small caps text links with slash separators. (2) Header CTA: same styling, position-differentiated only. (3) Row actions: "BUY TICKETS →" uppercase + trailing arrow, no underline, on cream panels. (4) Inline form submit: "SUBSCRIBE →" sharing the input underline. (5) Footer sitemap: display-serif slash-separated links. (6) Footer social/legal: small caps and small sentence-case text links. Emphasis is expressed by size tier and arrow glyphs, never by fills or underlines.
- **Observed vs inferred component evidence:** Observed real UI: nav, text links, arrow actions, info card, ruled price list, underline form, footer stacks, counter label. Inferred only: image carousel (from "1/6" counter; no controls visible). Not observed: filled/outlined buttons, tags/badges (except corner template badge), tabs, accordions, boxed inputs.
- **Component color adaptation evidence:** Text flips between near-black on cream and cream/white on dark browns; the yellow accent appears only for the logo on dark surfaces (header, footer) and the hero title — never on cream. Arrow actions remain dark-on-cream within child panels even inside dark sections.
- **Imagery / graphic evidence:** Dominant: warm-toned architectural/gallery photography as hard-edged, unframed, clipped rectangles — foreground-media. Exceptions: one full-bleed background-scale photo band (hard edges); grayscale map image background-contained within its rectangle; ghost typographic watermarks background-blended with seamless edges. One photo (curved interior) is reused in two sections.
- **Decoration placement evidence:** Two decorative systems: (1) giant ghost serif words/numerals anchored across full section width behind content, background-scale, deliberately overlapped by foreground media/text, legibility kept via extreme low contrast; (2) overlap mechanics as ornament — text over media, media over media, card over map, image over section seam. Small accents: slash separators, arrow glyphs, hairline rules. No blobs, glows, meshes, or angled elements.
