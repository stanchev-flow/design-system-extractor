WoodWave Gallery

A warm, two-tone editorial system for a photography gallery. Signature motifs: a single high-contrast didone serif set uppercase at every display tier; a cream canvas with deep warm-brown bookend bands; enormous ghost watermark words behind content; a staggered photographic collage with marginal micro-captions; and a muted gold accent reserved for dark surfaces.
