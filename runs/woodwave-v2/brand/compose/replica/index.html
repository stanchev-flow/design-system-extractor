About

Space as a muse: unleash your creativity and get inspired with us

At WoodWave Gallery, we hold a profound belief in the transformative influence of artistic environments on the boundless human imagination. Explore our one-of-a-kind space, where innovation and inspiration harmoniously converge, and set out on an enriching journey towards infinite creativity.

WoodWave Gallery is a tribute to decades of creative excellence, fostering the flourishing of art from the late 20th century to the present day

Founded by the visionary Margaret Woodwave in 1974, the WoodWave Gallery has been on a journey of constant evolution. Setting itself apart through the seamless blend of traditional and innovative artistic approaches, it has blossomed into a cultural landmark.
