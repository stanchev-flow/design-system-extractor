# WoodWave Gallery — Voice

Prose companion to `voice-facts.yaml` (the machine-checkable layer). Derived from the
captured copy only (section-copy.yaml + evidence/grounding).

## Register

Curatorial and evocative — the voice of a gallery that believes in art's power to
change how you see. It speaks in the **first person plural** ("we hold a profound
belief", "our one-of-a-kind space", "our crowning jewel") and invites the reader
INTO the space ("explore", "immerse yourself", "set out on an enriching journey").
Sentences are long and flowing; they reach for the transcendent — creativity,
imagination, inspiration, boundlessness — over the transactional.

## Patterns

- **Invitational openers.** Headings pose the space as a muse and invite creativity
  ("Space as a muse: unleash your creativity and get inspired with us").
- **Heritage as narrative.** The founder story is told as a journey ("Founded by the
  visionary Margaret Woodwave in 1974… a journey of constant evolution").
- **Evocative, not corporate.** The lexicon is romantic (transformative, boundless,
  harmoniously, seamless, immerse, crowning jewel). It NEVER reaches for SaaS jargon
  (leverage, ROI, funnel, scalable) — those break the register instantly.
- **Practical when it must be.** The visit band drops the poetry for plain facts
  (address, open hours, ticket prices) — clarity where the visitor needs it.
- **Typographic CTAs.** Actions are quiet and verb-led: "Buy tickets", "Subscribe" —
  uppercase text-arrow links, never shouty.

## Do

- Write long, flowing sentences that evoke the experience of the space.
- Speak as "we"; invite the reader as "you".
- Keep display headings SENTENCE case in the source — the type roles render them
  uppercase.
- Drop into plain, factual copy for the practical band (hours, prices, address).

## Avoid

- Corporate/SaaS jargon (leverage, synergy, ROI, KPI, funnel, scalable, turnkey).
- Exclamation marks and hype punctuation — the register is composed, not loud.
- Short choppy marketing sentences — the gallery reads unhurried.
