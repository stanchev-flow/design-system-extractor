# voice.md — HubSpot

> Canonical voice doc referenced by `brand.yaml > voice.ref`. Source: brand-layout-analyst
> Phase 2/3 reading of the reused HubSpot inventory (`docs/design-system-inventory/hubspot.com/`).

## Tone
Confident, optimistic, plain-spoken B2B SaaS. Speaks to operators ("grow your
business") not to art-world insiders. Sentences are short, benefit-led, and
specific ("resolve over 65% of customer inquiries"). Product names are
trademarked and capitalized (Marketing Hub®, Breeze Agents). No irony, no
editorial mystique — the opposite of WoodWave's terse gallery captions.

## Dials (locked — see brand.yaml)
- **variance: low** — one consistent system reused across every section; sections
  differ by content, not by reinventing the layout language.
- **motion: medium** — carousels, rotating logo SVGs, hover elevation on cards and
  buttons; motion is functional UI feedback, not decorative.
- **density: medium-high** — multi-column feature grids, dense footers, lots of
  interface chrome (cards, badges, tabs, metrics blocks).

## Copy rules
- Headlines are sentence case, never ALL CAPS (caps is reserved for short eyebrows).
- Every major section earns a real call-to-action button ("Get a demo",
  "Get started free", "Learn more"); actions are buttons/links, not bare arrows.
- Lead with the customer outcome, then name the product that delivers it.
