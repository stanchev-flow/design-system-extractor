QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

Know what sits behind every global hire

The important difference is operational: who owns the entity, who runs payroll, and where your employment data lives.

How does Employer of Record work?

Remote employs your team member through a local Remote entity and handles the employment contract, payroll, taxes, benefits, and compliance.

Can Remote run global payroll?

Yes. Remote supports multi-country payroll through the same platform, backed by in-house payroll operations and local expertise.

What about contractors?

Remote supports contractor management and Contractor of Record workflows, including contracts, payments, and compliance support.

How is employment data protected?

Remote states that its platform is SOC 2 Type 2 compliant, ISO 27001 certified, and GDPR compliant.
